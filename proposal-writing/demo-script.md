# 90-second walkthrough

Record only this public folder. Show only the published descriptions and conceptual diagram; keep private accounts, implementation and records off screen.

1. **0–18 seconds:** Explain why proposal accuracy matters after discovery.
2. **18–36 seconds:** Show the high-level notes-to-review-to-draft flow.
3. **36–54 seconds:** Explain how scope exclusions and unapproved commercial terms are handled.
4. **54–72 seconds:** Explain why the presentation remains a draft until a salesperson approves its contents.
5. **72–90 seconds:** State the verified seven-slide result and the known risk of omitted exclusions.

Use “designed to” for unmeasured commercial benefits. Do not imply that a demonstration produced meetings, revenue or customer acceptance.
