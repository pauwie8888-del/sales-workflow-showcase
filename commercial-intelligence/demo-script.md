# 90-second walkthrough

Record only this public folder. Use the mock illustration rather than a private account, workflow editor or customer record.

1. **0–18 seconds:** Explain the account-selection problem and show the status statement.
2. **18–36 seconds:** Open the fictional report. Point out the distinction between fit, timing and need.
3. **36–54 seconds:** Show the architecture diagram and explain that evidence can lead to a hold.
4. **54–72 seconds:** Explain the single follow-up research limit at a business level.
5. **72–90 seconds:** Finish with the live-versus-mock distinction and the pending independent benchmark.

Use “designed to” for unmeasured commercial benefits. Do not imply that a demonstration produced meetings, revenue or customer acceptance.
